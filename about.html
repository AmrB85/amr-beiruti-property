My approach

A property decision should begin with understanding, not pressure.

Buying a home and investing in property are different decisions. Each requires its own questions, research and evaluation. I believe clients should understand the property, the location, the costs and the risks before moving forward.

Understanding the Client

The right starting point is understanding whether you are buying a home, investing for rental income, planning for long-term growth or purchasing for future use.

Research Before Recommendations

Location, property type, developer, building quality, payment structure, ongoing costs and resale considerations all deserve attention.

Clear Investment Thinking

Rental income should be considered alongside service charges, maintenance, vacancy periods, financing costs and other expenses. Gross yield is not the same as net return.

Support Beyond the Purchase

A property relationship should not end when the transaction is completed. Post-sales guidance can help clients navigate practical next steps after purchase.
