01 / DUBAI

Dubai Property Market Guide

A large and varied market with established communities, new developments, apartments, villas and different investment strategies.

Areas to Research

Different locations serve different needs. Examples worth researching include:

Dubai Marina Downtown Dubai Dubai Hills Estate Business Bay Dubai Creek Harbour Jumeirah Village Circle Palm Jumeirah

Compare actual buildings and individual units, not just community names. Two properties in the same area can have very different costs, views, layouts and rental performance.

Ownership Considerations

Non-UAE nationals may acquire freehold ownership in designated areas, as well as certain usufruct and leasehold rights. Confirm the precise plot, unit and ownership type with Dubai Land Department before signing.

Dubai Land Department

For Home Buyers

  • Compare commute times and access to schools, healthcare and daily services.
  • Inspect building maintenance, facilities, parking and service charges.
  • Review the title deed, seller authority and any mortgage or restrictions.
  • For off-plan units, understand the payment schedule, completion terms and handover obligations.

For Property Investors

  • Compare achievable rent with similar units in the same building or community.
  • Calculate net income after service charges, maintenance, vacancy and management costs.
  • Assess competing supply and the resale market.
  • Do not assume advertised rental yields are guaranteed.
02 / ABU DHABI

Abu Dhabi Property Market Guide

Abu Dhabi offers a distinct property market, with investment zones, established residential districts, island communities and a range of apartment and villa developments.

Areas to Research

Yas Island Saadiyat Island Al Reem Island Al Raha Beach Al Reef Masdar City Al Maryah Island

Research transport, schools, employment centres, amenities, building quality and the type of residents likely to rent or buy there.

Ownership Considerations

Abu Dhabi law allows non-UAE nationals to own and acquire real rights in properties located within designated investment areas. Verify the exact project, land status and ownership structure with the Abu Dhabi Real Estate Centre.

Abu Dhabi Real Estate Centre

For Home Buyers

  • Compare island living with mainland residential communities.
  • Consider commuting, schools, healthcare and access to work.
  • Inspect the unit, building facilities and community management.
  • Understand the ownership rights attached to the specific property.

For Property Investors

  • Study rental demand for the specific community and property type.
  • Compare service charges and property management costs.
  • Assess new supply and competing developments.
  • Use realistic rental comparisons rather than promotional estimates.
03 / SHARJAH

Sharjah Property Market Guide

Sharjah has a distinct residential market, with established neighbourhoods, newer master-planned communities, waterfront developments and family-oriented locations.

Areas to Research

Aljada Muwaileh Tilal City Maryam Island Al Khan Al Majaz Sharjah Waterfront City

Compare access to schools, universities, employment areas, Dubai connections, public facilities and the character of each development.

Ownership Considerations

Sharjah has approved development areas and projects where all nationalities may acquire freehold ownership, subject to the applicable requirements. Do not assume every property in Sharjah qualifies. Confirm the project and unit with the relevant registration authority.

Sharjah Executive Council

For Home Buyers

  • Compare apartment and villa options according to family needs.
  • Check school access, daily travel and community facilities.
  • Verify the permitted ownership type for your nationality and project.
  • Review building condition, service charges and maintenance arrangements.

For Property Investors

  • Research tenant demand for the exact building and unit type.
  • Compare achievable rent with total acquisition and running costs.
  • Assess resale demand and competing supply.
  • Check developer approvals and project registration for off-plan purchases.
Ownership eligibility is project-specific. Older online information may describe foreign ownership as usufruct only. Check the current approved project rules and registration requirements.
04 / RAS AL KHAIMAH

Ras Al Khaimah Property Market Guide

Ras Al Khaimah offers a different mix of residential communities, coastal locations, resort-related developments and investment opportunities. Assess the specific project and long-term demand rather than relying on destination marketing alone.

Areas to Research

Al Marjan Island Mina Al Arab Al Hamra Village RAK Central Julphar Towers Al Maareedh

Consider proximity to beaches, employment, schools, healthcare, shopping, transport and services available outside peak tourist periods.

Ownership Considerations

Ras Al Khaimah has designated freehold areas where buyers of different nationalities may acquire ownership, subject to applicable rules. Confirm the exact plot and unit eligibility with the competent land registration authority.

RAK Government Services

For Home Buyers

  • Decide whether you prefer a coastal, resort-style or established residential community.
  • Check year-round services, access to schools and healthcare.
  • Review building management, maintenance and community charges.
  • Understand whether the property is residential, hotel-managed or subject to special operating arrangements.

For Property Investors

  • Separate residential rental demand from short-stay tourism demand.
  • Check any hotel or rental management agreement and its fees.
  • Assess competing projects, future supply and delivery schedules.
  • Stress-test income assumptions for lower occupancy and seasonal changes.
A waterfront address or tourism connection does not guarantee rental income or capital growth. Review the actual contract, operating costs, restrictions and comparable transactions.
Before you buy

A Practical UAE Property Due-Diligence Checklist

Regardless of which emirate you choose, the following questions can help you avoid overlooking important details.

01. Ownership
Confirm the title, ownership category, eligibility and registration authority.
02. Seller and developer
Verify the seller's authority and the developer's legal status and project approvals.
03. Total purchase cost
Calculate registration, agency, legal, mortgage and other applicable fees.
04. Service charges
Request the actual service charge schedule and check outstanding balances.
05. Property condition
Arrange an inspection and review defects, maintenance and building condition.
06. Rental assumptions
Compare achievable rent with real comparable properties, not advertised promises.
07. Resale and exit
Consider buyer demand, liquidity, competing supply and possible selling costs.
08. Contract review
Understand payment terms, completion dates, default provisions and handover obligations.
Investment fundamentals

Understand Gross Yield and Net Return

Gross Rental Yield

Annual rent ÷ Purchase price × 100

This is a starting point only. It does not account for operating expenses, vacancy, financing or other costs.

Net Operating Return

(Annual rent − operating expenses) ÷ Total investment cost × 100

Use realistic rent and include service charges, maintenance, management, vacancy and other relevant expenses.
