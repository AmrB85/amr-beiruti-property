Think Beyond the Listing

Market Intelligence

A property decision should consider more than the advertised price. Explore the main areas of research that can help buyers evaluate an opportunity.

01 · Location

The right location for the objective.

Consider connectivity, nearby employment centres, schools, retail, transport, future infrastructure and the type of tenant or buyer likely to be attracted to the area.

02 · Developer

Research the developer.

Review delivery history, project quality, construction progress, reputation, contract terms and the specific project—not just the developer's marketing.

03 · Costs

Understand the full cost.

Look beyond the purchase price. Consider applicable registration fees, agency fees, service charges, financing costs, maintenance and other ownership expenses.

04 · Exit Strategy

Think about the next buyer.

Consider resale demand, competing supply, property layout, building condition, ownership costs and how easily the property may suit another buyer.

Rental Yield

Gross yield is only the starting point.

Gross rental yield is annual rent divided by purchase price. Net yield should account for relevant ownership expenses, vacancy, management, maintenance and other costs.

Off-Plan

Payment plans need careful review.

Understand construction milestones, payment dates, handover expectations, contract conditions, project registration and the financial implications if circumstances change.

Financing

Understand your actual buying capacity.

Consider deposit requirements, mortgage eligibility, interest rates, monthly commitments, fees and the cash reserve you need after completion.

Risk

A good opportunity still carries risk.

Market conditions, supply, construction delays, rental demand, financing and resale liquidity can affect the outcome. No projected return is guaranteed.

Before choosing a property

Define your objective, budget, preferred location, property type, intended holding period and whether you plan to live in or rent out the property.

Before making an offer

Verify ownership and title details, property condition, comparable transactions, outstanding charges, service fees and all relevant contract terms.

Before buying off-plan

Check project registration, escrow arrangements, construction status, payment schedule, completion terms and the sale and purchase agreement.

Before taking ownership

Plan inspection, snagging, handover documentation, utility connections, service charges, maintenance and any leasing arrangements.

Useful Questions

Before you make a decision.

Some important questions buyers should consider when researching Dubai property.

What is the difference between gross and net rental yield?
Gross yield compares annual rent with the purchase price. Net yield takes relevant expenses into account, such as service charges, maintenance, management, vacancy and other ownership costs. Always compare like-for-like figures.
What should I check before buying off-plan?
Review the developer, project registration, escrow arrangements, construction progress, payment schedule, sale and purchase agreement, handover terms and the total financial commitment.
Does a strong Dubai market mean every property is a good investment?
No. Performance varies by location, building, developer, purchase price, supply, rental demand and ownership costs. A strong overall market does not remove property-specific risks.
Should I use a developer payment plan or a mortgage?
It depends on the property, your financial position, eligibility, payment dates, interest costs and cash-flow requirements. Compare the total cost and commitments rather than focusing only on the monthly payment.
What should I check before accepting a ready property?
Inspect the unit and building, review title and ownership documents, check service charges and outstanding amounts, confirm included items and document any defects before handover.
