Frequently Asked Questions

Questions worth asking.

Is Dubai property suitable for every investor?

No investment is suitable for everyone. The right choice depends on budget, objectives, time horizon, risk tolerance and the specific property.

Should I buy off-plan or a ready property?

Both options have different considerations. Off-plan purchases require careful review of the developer, construction timeline and payment terms. Ready properties allow buyers to assess the actual asset and current rental position.

What costs should I consider beyond the purchase price?

Consider transfer and registration fees, agency fees where applicable, mortgage costs, service charges, maintenance, furnishing and other ownership expenses.

Can you help me compare different areas?

Yes. Area selection should consider your budget, intended use, lifestyle priorities, access, supply and the type of property you are considering.
