COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Can foreign buyers purchase property in Dubai?

Foreign buyers can purchase freehold property in designated areas. The permitted ownership structure and requirements should be checked for the specific property.

Is off-plan property always a better investment?

No. Off-plan and ready properties have different benefits and risks. Compare the developer, project, location, payment plan, completion timeline and your own financial position.

What costs should I consider when buying?

Depending on the transaction, costs may include transfer and registration fees, agency fees, mortgage expenses, trustee fees, service charges and other applicable costs.

Can you help after I purchase a property?

Yes. I can help guide you through the next steps and connect you with relevant service providers for handover, leasing and property management, depending on your needs.
