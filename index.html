Buyer Resource Centre

Buying property in the UAE: what should you check?

Use this practical overview to prepare before viewing, negotiating or committing to a property. Requirements can vary by emirate, property and transaction.

01

Define your objective

Are you buying a home, a holiday property, a long-term investment or a property for rental income? Your objective changes what you should prioritise.

  • Your preferred location and lifestyle
  • Your intended holding period
  • Your budget and payment capacity
02

Calculate the full cost

The advertised property price is not always the total amount you need. Ask for a written breakdown of transaction and ownership costs.

  • Registration and transfer-related charges
  • Agency, trustee and administrative fees where applicable
  • Mortgage, valuation and bank costs if applicable
  • Service charges, maintenance and furnishing
03

Verify before committing

Confirm the legal and practical position of the property through the relevant official channels and qualified professionals.

  • Ownership and title documentation
  • Property status and any registered restrictions
  • Outstanding charges and occupancy
  • Contract terms and payment obligations
Important: This is general educational information, not legal, tax, mortgage or financial advice. Confirm current procedures, fees and eligibility with the relevant authority, bank or qualified adviser before making a decision.
Investor Education

A property investment should be measured, not assumed.

Rental income and price growth are never guaranteed. A useful assessment looks at the complete financial picture, including costs, risks and the investor's time horizon.

Gross rental yield

A simple starting point for comparing properties. It does not include ownership expenses or periods without a tenant.

Gross yield =
Annual expected rent ÷ purchase price × 100

Net rental yield

A more useful estimate because it accounts for relevant recurring expenses. Make sure the calculation uses realistic assumptions.

Net yield =
(Annual rent − annual operating costs) ÷ total acquisition cost × 100

What should an investor compare?

Factor Questions to ask
Purchase price How does it compare with similar properties in the same location and condition?
Rental demand Who is the likely tenant, and what comparable rental evidence is available?
Running costs What are the service charges, maintenance costs, management fees and other expenses?
Vacancy risk What happens to cash flow if the property remains vacant or rent is lower than expected?
Resale and liquidity Who might buy this property later, and how easy could it be to sell?
Risk and time horizon Can you hold the property if the market changes or your plans are delayed?
Investor reminder: A projected yield is not a guaranteed return. Verify rental comparables, actual service charges, property condition, transaction costs and current market evidence before investing.
Off-Plan Property Guide

Before you reserve an off-plan property

A payment plan is only one part of an off-plan purchase. Understand the project, contractual obligations and delivery risks before committing.

Check the project

  • Verify the project and developer through the relevant authority.
  • Understand the project's registration and escrow arrangements where applicable.
  • Review the location, surrounding development and access.

Read the contract

  • Payment milestones and due dates.
  • Expected completion and handover provisions.
  • Default, delay and cancellation clauses.
  • Unit specifications and permitted changes.

Plan for handover

  • Understand inspection and snagging arrangements.
  • Budget for furnishing and connection costs.
  • Clarify service charges and ongoing obligations.
Ready Property Guide

A viewing is the beginning of due diligence, not the end.

A property can look attractive during a viewing and still have issues that matter financially or legally. Use a structured review.

During the property inspection

  • Check plumbing, air conditioning and electrical systems.
  • Look for moisture, leaks, cracks and visible damage.
  • Review noise, natural light, views and privacy.
  • Inspect common areas, lifts, parking and building maintenance.

Before transfer

  • Verify ownership and title documentation.
  • Check property status and any restrictions.
  • Confirm service-charge and utility positions.
  • Confirm vacant or tenanted status and relevant arrangements.
  • Have the transaction documents reviewed where necessary.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions buyers and investors often ask.

What should I consider before buying property in Dubai?

Start with your objective, budget and preferred location. Then compare similar properties, understand all acquisition and ownership costs, verify ownership and property status, and review the contract before committing.

Is off-plan or ready property better?

Neither is automatically better. Off-plan purchases require careful review of the project, developer, contract, payment plan and delivery risk. Ready properties allow inspection of the existing unit and building, but still require ownership, condition and cost checks.

How do I calculate rental yield?

Gross yield is annual rent divided by purchase price, multiplied by 100. Net yield should account for relevant recurring costs and use the total acquisition cost as its basis. Neither figure guarantees future income.

Can overseas buyers purchase property in the UAE?

Eligibility and ownership rules depend on the emirate, location, property and buyer circumstances. Confirm the applicable rules with the relevant land department or a qualified legal professional before paying a deposit.

What are service charges?

Service charges are recurring charges associated with maintaining and operating a building or community. Ask for the actual applicable charges for the specific unit and understand what is included.

What happens after I purchase a property?

Depending on the property, next steps may include transfer or handover documentation, utility connections, snagging, furnishing, leasing arrangements and ongoing service-charge payments. Confirm the relevant process for your transaction.
