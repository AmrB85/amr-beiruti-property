WHY DUBAI · THE GLOBAL PICTURE

A global property market with measurable momentum.

Dubai is located on the southeast coast of the Arabian Peninsula, in the United Arab Emirates, along the southern shore of the Arabian Gulf. Its position between Europe, Asia and Africa, together with its aviation links, business environment and international population, has helped it become a major global city for business, tourism and real estate.

What do these numbers tell us?

Dubai's government reported more than 270,000 real-estate transactions worth AED 917 billion in 2025, a 20% increase in value year on year. Real-estate investments exceeded AED 680 billion across approximately 258,600 deals. The investor base reached around 193,100.

These are broad real-estate sector figures. They include more than residential home sales alone. Knight Frank separately reported 205,400 residential sales worth AED 544.2 billion in 2025. The datasets measure different categories and should not be confused.

Sources: Dubai Government · 12 January 2026 · Knight Frank · 2025 residential review

How does Dubai compare internationally?

Savills' World Cities Prime Residential Index reported that Dubai was among five leading cities with prime residential capital-value growth above 6% during 2025. The other cities named in that leading group were Tokyo, Seoul, Amsterdam and Cape Town. This is a prime residential comparison, not a ranking of every property market or every home type.

WHAT INVESTORS SHOULD CONSIDER

Growth is not a guarantee.

A strong market-wide result does not mean every building, developer, location or purchase price is a good investment. Buyers still need to examine supply, future handovers, service charges, rental demand, resale liquidity, financing costs and their own holding period.

PROPERTY KNOWLEDGE

Questions buyers should ask.

Straightforward explanations of important Dubai property topics. These are general educational notes, not legal, tax, mortgage or investment advice.

What is the difference between gross and net rental yield?

Gross yield is annual rent divided by the purchase price. Net yield deducts relevant ownership and operating expenses, such as service charges, maintenance, management, insurance and vacancy. Always compare like with like and state which costs are included.

What should I check before buying off-plan?

Check the project's registration, developer details, escrow arrangements, construction progress, payment milestones, handover provisions, service charges and assignment or resale rules. Review the actual contract and verify important details with the relevant official authority.

Does a high transaction volume mean every area is a good investment?

No. Transaction volume indicates activity, not guaranteed future performance. Compare price per square foot, property type, supply pipeline, rental demand, service charges, building quality, liquidity and the price you are paying.

What is the difference between an off-plan payment plan and a mortgage?

A developer payment plan sets out instalments payable under the purchase contract. A mortgage is lending from a bank or finance provider and has its own eligibility rules, interest or profit rate, fees and repayment obligations. Confirm the full cash requirement and timing before committing.

What should I review when buying a ready property?

Verify ownership and title details, inspect the property, review tenancy status and rental income, check service-charge statements, understand transfer costs and compare recent similar transactions. Use qualified legal and technical professionals where appropriate.
